You hold NIM or USDT. They accept Bitcoin over Lightning.

LightningBridge connects those networks inside Nimiq Pay. The recipient keeps their Lightning wallet.

Pay a Lightning Address and amount, or paste a fixed-amount invoice. No new recipient account is needed.

For an address, LightningBridge requests a fresh invoice when you are ready to pay. Reuse the address next time.

Review the amount and fees in Nimiq Pay. Choose NIM or USDT, then approve in your wallet.

After approval, Nimiq Pay handles the self-custodial swap to Bitcoin over Lightning.

Pay up to twenty invoices from one list. Approve each payment separately.

Submitted is not settled. Check the recipient's Lightning wallet for confirmation.
