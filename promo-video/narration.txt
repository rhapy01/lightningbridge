You hold NIM or USDT. The person you are paying accepts Bitcoin over Lightning.

LightningBridge connects those two payment methods inside Nimiq Pay. The recipient keeps using their existing Lightning wallet.

Enter a Lightning Address and amount, or paste a fixed-amount invoice. You do not need a Lightning wallet, and the recipient does not need a new account.

For an address, LightningBridge requests a fresh invoice for your chosen amount at checkout. The address remains reusable; each payment gets a new invoice.

Review the request in Nimiq Pay. Choose NIM or USDT, check the amount and fees, then approve in your wallet.

After approval, Nimiq Pay handles the self-custodial swap from your chosen asset to Bitcoin over Lightning.

Pay several people from one list with up to twenty invoices. Each payment still needs its own approval.

Submitted does not mean settled. Check the recipient's Lightning wallet for confirmation. LightningBridge: open it inside Nimiq Pay.
